Project Overview

Government office renovation in Kismayo

The Kismayo Municipal Offices project upgraded an existing government building for safer, more efficient day-to-day use. Anchor led structural assessment, strengthening works and a full renovation package.

Work included repairs to the frame and finishes, improved services routing, and quality control so staff could return to a building that meets current standards.

Scope of work

  • Structural condition survey and strengthening
  • Full interior and exterior renovation
  • Services coordination during upgrade
  • Final inspection and handover
