Bosaso Link Bridge officially opens to the public

After months of civil engineering works, the Bosaso Link Bridge has officially opened to the public. The pedestrian and light-vehicle crossing connects two previously divided districts, cutting commute times and giving residents safer access across the channel it spans.

Anchor's civil engineering team led the site works and inspection for the project, working alongside local contractors to deliver the crossing on a compressed timeline ahead of the rainy season.

Built for the conditions

The design accounts for seasonal flooding and heavy foot traffic, with reinforced approach ramps and a deck engineered to carry pedestrians, motorbikes and light vehicles safely in all weather.

"A bridge like this changes daily life immediately — people can see and feel the difference the same week it opens."

Anchor continues to work with local and regional authorities on infrastructure projects that connect communities. Get in touch if your district has a similar project in mind.
