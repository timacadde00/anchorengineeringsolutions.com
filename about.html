Our Story

A practice built one project at a time

Anchor Engineering Solutions was founded to close a simple gap: too many building projects in the region were losing money and safety margin to poor supervision. We started as a small structural consultancy and grew into a full-service civil and construction management practice, without ever handing site oversight to someone other than a qualified engineer.

Today our team supports government, private and residential clients — from a single-storey renovation to multi-building infrastructure programmes — with the same document discipline and site presence on every job.

Inside Anchor

Who we are

Anchor is a leader in many diversified markets with a focus on infrastructure, environmental, energy, water and waste water treatment. Anchor delivers design/design-build, program and project management services. We pride ourselves in our ability to listen to clients and understand their needs.

We work with our clients to determine the most appropriate procurement solution for their project and treat as our highest priority. We make a real difference to citizens and communities by facilitating positive, lasting change and enabling excellence in service delivery.

Our corporate responsibility is about approaching our business aims with an awareness of the associated social and environmental implications, and taking measures to address these in a responsible and sustainable manner.

Our Mission

Anchor aims to produce high quality engineering design in concept, in detail and in execution, on time, to programme and delivering excellent value for money. To develop innovative, economic and sustainable solutions that successfully meet the needs of our clients, whilst adding value and a better quality of life for all.

Our Vision

We treat our clients and each other with respect. We are honest and say what we mean. We encourage open debate, and if we think someone is making a mistake, we say so, tactfully. We do not play political games. Once a decision is made, we are committed to following through.

Our Values

We are thoughtful. We understand the value of experience, but we also question traditional approaches, looking for better, smarter ways to do things — the answer "we've always done it that way" is not enough.
