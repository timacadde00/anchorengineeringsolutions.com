Project Overview

Residential development in Mogadishu

This 6-storey residential apartment building in Mogadishu has been completed, delivering modern, well-ventilated living spaces across a 1,380 sqm built-up area. Anchor provided structural design, construction management and site supervision from foundation to finishing.

Each typical floor holds two spacious apartment units, each with a lounge, two bedrooms, kitchen, bathroom and private balcony. The building is topped with a covered terrace offering shared outdoor space and open sky views.

Scope of work

  • Structural design and reinforcement detailing
  • Full construction management, foundation to finishing
  • Site supervision and quality control
  • Coordination of MEP works
