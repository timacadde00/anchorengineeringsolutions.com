Aden Business Complex

Structural design and site supervision for a 6-storey mixed-use commercial building, including full reinforcement detailing.

Project Overview

Mixed-use commercial structure in Hargeisa

Aden Business Complex is a 6-storey mixed-use commercial building in Hargeisa. Anchor provided the full structural design package and on-site supervision so the frame, floors and cores were built to the approved drawings.

The scope covered reinforcement detailing for columns, beams and slabs, coordination with architectural layouts, and quality checks during pouring and curing. The result is a durable commercial frame ready for fit-out.

Scope of work

  • Structural analysis and member sizing
  • Full reinforcement detailing drawings
  • Site supervision during structural works
  • Coordination with architectural and MEP teams
