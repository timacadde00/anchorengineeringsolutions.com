Project Overview

Office tower delivery in Mogadishu

Coastal Heights Tower is a 9-storey office building in Mogadishu delivered under Anchor construction management. The team coordinated contractors, programme, cost control and site safety from foundation through to final handover.

Weekly progress reporting, inspection of structural and finishing works, and close-out documentation kept the client informed and the handover package complete.

Scope of work

  • Programme and contractor coordination
  • Cost control and progress reporting
  • Structural and finishing inspections
  • Handover documentation and snagging
