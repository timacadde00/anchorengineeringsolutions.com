Bosaso Link Bridge

Civil engineering, site works and inspection for a pedestrian and light-vehicle crossing.

Project Overview

Pedestrian and light-vehicle crossing in Bosaso

Bosaso Link Bridge provides a safer crossing for pedestrians and light vehicles. Anchor handled civil engineering design support, site works coordination and inspection through construction.

Approach works, deck finishes and safety detailing were checked against the approved drawings so the crossing could open for everyday use with confidence.

Scope of work

  • Civil design support and setting-out checks
  • Site works coordination
  • Inspection of structural and approach works
  • Safety and finish verification before opening
