Project Overview

Duplex residential villa

Farey Villa is a completed duplex residential house measuring 20m × 15m. The design places two mirrored living units under one roof, sharing a distinctive central chimney feature that gives the elevation its character.

Anchor delivered architectural coordination, structural design and site supervision through to handover. The villa is now occupied and stands as an example of compact, well-finished residential work.

Scope of work

  • Architectural layout and duplex unit planning
  • Structural design for load-bearing walls and roof
  • Site supervision through finishing and handover
  • Quality control on finishes and openings
