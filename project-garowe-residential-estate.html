Garowe Residential Estate

Structural design and quality control for a 40-unit residential housing development.

Project Overview

40-unit housing development in Garowe

Garowe Residential Estate is a 40-unit housing development where Anchor provided structural design and ongoing quality control. Consistent detailing across the estate kept construction efficient and the finished homes reliable.

The team reviewed foundations, frames and slab works at key stages, flagging issues early so units could progress without costly rework.

Scope of work

  • Structural design for typical housing units
  • Foundation and frame quality control
  • Site inspections at key construction stages
  • Coordination with site contractor teams
